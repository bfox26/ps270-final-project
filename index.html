Proposal Paragraph

Does a WNBA’s team opponent influence the amount of people who attend games? I hypothesize that the team’s opponent will not influence the amount of people who attend games because the WNBA often has player versus player rivalries instead of team versus team rivalries. This has been seen more recently especially with the popularity of Caitlyn Clark, from Indiana Fever, and Sabrina Ionescu, from New York Liberty. In the dataset we can see that in 2019 Indiana Fever attendance was relatively low; the highest number of attendees were around 3,000 people. When Caitlyn Clark became more popular in 2024 the number of attendees rose to around 6,000 people. My data set includes information about WNBA attendance from 1994 to 2024 across all 23 teams that have been in operation throughout this twenty year period. My first explanatory variable of interest is the opponent which is measured by who the team was playing. The second explanatory variable is year which is measured by what year the game was played. This is informative because this will help me understand how attendance has increased and decreased over time because the WNBA has become more known. The outcome variable of interest is attendance which is measured by how many people attended each game. I do not know how this was measured in the dataset but my guess is it was measured by how many people purchased tickets. My hypothesis will be disproven if there is a higher amount of attendees in specific seasons for two teams who played against each other. My hypothesis will be proven if there are overall similarities between the means of attendance when certain teams play against each other.

Introduction

Does a team being a member of the WNBA since it’s founding in 1997 increase home game attendance? The four WNBA teams that were added to the league in 1997 and are still league members in 2026 are: the Houston Comets, Los Angeles Sparks, Phoenix Mercury, and the New York Liberty. I hypothesize that the New York Liberty will have the highest home game attendance because New York City is the largest city compared to Houston, LA, and Phoenix. The WNBA was founded 29 years ago. Although this is true for all of the four teams that were first included in the league in 1997 and still exist today, the fact that New York City is much larger than the other cities give team management the ability to reach more fans. Meaning it is more likely that the New York Liberty would always have more fans at home games because they have more fans to gain.

This question is important because the WNBA is under researched. Since it is a relative new league there are few data sets and historical information available about the league. Unlike the NBA which has been around for 77 years and is well known by the general public. Given that this research question is focused on an all women league sexism plays a role. This fact makes this research question more interesting and important to analyze. It would be assumed that the teams that were originally in the league would receive higher attendance than the teams that were more reccently added to the league.

The dependent variable in my analysis is attendance which is measured by how many people attend games. This includes games during the regular season, championships, and all star games, which are when most valued players of each team play a 1v1 game against other teams most valued players. The data set that I am drawing my values from is a cross sectional data set because the data draws information from different variables including: Day, Year, Game Type, Attendance, Home Team, Away Team, Arena, City, and State.

The explanatory variables, are the four teams that were originally included in the WNBA league and that are still in the league in 2026. These teams are: the Los Angeles Sparks, Phoenix Mercury, Houston Comets, and New York Liberty. The second explanatory variable are the four teams that were most recently added to the WNBA, in the 2000s. These four teams are: the Dallas Wings, Las Vegas Aces, Seattle Storm, and Atlanta Dream. In my analysis I refer to these teams as “Home teams” because I am drawing the values from the data set that include the attendance at games where those eight teams are playing at their home arena. The criteria for supporting my hypothesis is if the New York Liberty have the highest attendance across the board, including the more recently added teams.
